## About Us

We are a family-owned artisan bakery dedicated to creating custom cakes that celebrate your most special moments. With over 8 years of experience, each cake is baked from scratch using premium ingredients and hand-decorated with love and attention to detail.

## Our Philosophy

We believe every celebration deserves a unique cake that tells your story. We work with you from design to delivery to ensure your cake is exactly as you dreamed it.

## Service Area

We offer home delivery in Downtown, North Side, and South Side areas. You can also pick up your order at our workshop. Contact us about additional areas.
