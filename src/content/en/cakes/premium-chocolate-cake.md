# Premium Chocolate Cake

An indulgent chocolate cake that combines moist layers of dark chocolate sponge with silky ganache that melts in your mouth. Golden touches add elegance to this irresistible creation.

## Design Details

- **Size**: 8 inches diameter
- **Flavors**: 70% dark chocolate with Belgian chocolate ganache
- **Decoration**: Mirror ganache with edible gold leaf details
- **Finish**: Smooth and glossy surface
- **Creation time**: 2 days

## Special Occasion

Perfect for birthday celebrations where chocolate is the star. This cake has been a favorite for intimate and elegant celebrations.
