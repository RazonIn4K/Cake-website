# Artisan Cupcakes

Delicious individual cupcakes decorated with handmade buttercream roses. Each cupcake is a small work of art, perfect for celebrations where variety and elegance are desired.

## Design Details

- **Quantity**: Available from 6 to 48 units
- **Flavors**: Vanilla, chocolate, red velvet, lemon
- **Decoration**: Buttercream roses in different colors
- **Finish**: Smooth buttercream with piping bag technique
- **Creation time**: 1 day

## Special Occasion

Ideal for baby showers, farewells, family gatherings or any celebration where individual portions with elegant presentation are desired.
