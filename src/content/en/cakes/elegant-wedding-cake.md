# Elegant Wedding Cake

This beautiful three-tier cake was created especially for an intimate wedding. The sugar flowers were hand-molded to complement the bride's bouquet, creating perfect harmony between all celebration elements.

## Design Details

- **Tiers**: Three levels with 10", 8" and 6" diameters
- **Flavors**: French vanilla with raspberry filling
- **Decoration**: Handcrafted sugar flowers in pink and white tones
- **Finish**: Smooth fondant with matte texture
- **Creation time**: 3 days

## Special Occasion

This cake was the centerpiece at a garden wedding, where the soft tones and natural elegance of the design perfectly complemented the romantic atmosphere of the event.
