# Local Business Information

## Our Location

We're located in the heart of the city, in an accessible location for the entire metropolitan area. We offer delivery services and you can also pick up your order directly from our workshop.

## Service Area

We deliver to:
- **Downtown**: No additional cost
- **North Side**: $5 delivery fee
- **South Side**: $5 delivery fee  
- **Surrounding Neighborhoods**: $7 delivery fee

For locations outside our service area, contact us to evaluate special options.

## Business Hours

We're available for consultations and orders during our operating hours. For urgent orders or after-hours consultations, you can contact us via WhatsApp.

## Order Policies

- **Standard Orders**: Require minimum 1 week advance notice
- **Wedding Cakes**: Require minimum 2 weeks advance notice  
- **Express Orders**: Available with additional charge (minimum 48 hours)
- **Deposit**: 50% deposit required to confirm all orders
